Game build: DayZ 1.30. Seen 2026-11-05 at 720p and 1440p.

## Decided
For a look check at another resolution, switch it in the game's own Video options while the client runs. No restart is needed and UI built from screen height re-lays out.

## Watch out
Calling a slider "player scale" makes testers think it changes the character's size; "UI scale" reads right.
